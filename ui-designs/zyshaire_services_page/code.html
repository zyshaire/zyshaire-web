Common Questions

How quickly can we start a project?

expand_more

Typically, we can kick off within 1-2 weeks after the discovery call and proposal approval. We start with a rapid onboarding phase to align on goals and access requirements.

Do you work with non-technical founders?

expand_more

Absolutely. We specialize in translating business outcomes into technical requirements. We act as your technical partner, explaining decisions in plain English, not jargon.

What is your tech stack?

expand_more

We are pragmatic. For web, we often use React/Next.js and Node.js or Python. For AI, we leverage OpenAI, Anthropic, and open-source models via LangChain. We choose the best tool for the job, not just the newest trend.

How do you handle IP rights?

expand_more

You own the code. Upon final payment, all intellectual property rights for custom development are transferred to your company.
